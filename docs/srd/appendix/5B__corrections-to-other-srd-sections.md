# Corrections to other SRD sections (from the 5B draft)

The investor portal was replaced on 30 Sep 2026 by the LP portal imported from the sibling fund-ERP product. Every statement in `docs/SRD.md` about the V65 / "LP Portal V59" investor prototype, its five pages, its legacy bridge and its `?client=` override is obsolete. The earlier draft `draft-5B-investor.md` and its "Corrections" block (written for the V65 page) are superseded by this block. The corrections below are cumulative to the SRD as it stands today.

- **1.4 Current implementation baseline and 2 System overview**

It makes no call to the Nexus API except sign-in, `GET /api/auth/me` and `POST /api/auth/logout`. The portal's `apiClient` is a stub that rejects.
  - The investor entry in `tools/legacy-bridge.mjs` `DISABLED`, `packages/legacy-runtime` and the `legacy/` assets are no longer used by the investor portal.
  - State that the LP API contract (70 methods, `lib/api/lp-portal-api.ts`) is the specification to implement in `backend/api`; the NVCCZ backend (`C:\Users\lysp\Downloads\nvccz`) is a reference implementation, not a dependency.
