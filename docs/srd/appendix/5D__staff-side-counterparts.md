# STAFF-SIDE COUNTERPARTS (from the 5D draft)

Principle: every investee page needs a staff action that produces or decides its data; without it the page can only show empty states. Placement: **Admin portal › Private Markets (5.A.3)**, company workspace drawer/page with tabs (Reporting, KPIs & covenants, Term sheet, Cap table, Governance, Requests, Documents, Messages, Team, Forecast). Permissions today: VIEW = `portfolio.view`, REVIEW = `research.edit` (reviewer ≠ submitter). Proposed new codes are listed at the end (candidate codes, decided with Q-04).

| Investee page | Staff must be able to | Where in the Nexus admin portal | Exists today (Nexus) | Proposed permission |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5.D.0 Shell | Link users to a company (first user), see which company users exist, disable access; configure notification templates and reminder defaults | Private Markets › company › Team; Settings (5.A.27) | Invite company user `POST /private-markets/companies/{ref}/users` | `portfolio.view` / `research.edit` |
| 5.D.1 Overview | See the same metrics, readiness and action items per company and across the portfolio; export | Private Markets list + company header | Companies list with latest period, open questions, covenant alerts | `portfolio.view` |
| 5.D.2 KPI Centre | Define KPIs per company (key, label, category, unit, frequency, owner title, target by period, tolerance, direction, definition, evidence requirement); lock/approve definition changes (portfolio lead); view submitted vs derived values; correct or restate with a reason | Private Markets › company › KPIs & covenants | Covenant PATCH only; `company_kpis` seed-only | `research.edit` (define), second approver for changes |
| 5.D.3 Reporting Centre | Open a reporting period with due date, period type and mandatory attachments (existing `POST …/periods`); nudge with a message; see read receipts; review each submitted report and the pack: accept, return (note required), request revision; add variance questions; maintain the report templates published to companies; open calendar across companies | Private Markets › Reporting packs queue and company › Reporting | Open period, packs list, accept/return (SoD), questions, covenants | `research.edit` (review), `portfolio.view` |
| 5.D.4 Forecast Model | Review submitted forecast versions (accept/return with comment); compare to prior version and board-approved plan; set minimum cash policy and which scenario is board-approved; lift a month lock with a reason | Private Markets › company › Forecast | None | `portfolio.forecast.review` |
| 5.D.5 Term Sheet | Create and edit the term sheet (title, amount, equity %, valuation, key terms, conditions, timeline, structured terms, key dates, document), finalise (notifies the company), sign as the fund (investor signatory), record execution/closing dates, withdraw/expire; publish governing documents | Private Markets › company › Term sheet; Document Vault (5.A.13) for governing documents | None | `portfolio.terms.issue`; fund signatory authority |
| 5.D.6 Cap Table | Bootstrap the registry; maintain share classes (code, type, par, votes, seniority, liquidation multiple, participation, authorised, conversion ratio), stakeholders (link LP clients), option grants, convertibles; create issuance/transfer, submit, approve, execute (GL posting, certificate); set "verified as at" date; publish to the company | Private Markets › company › Cap table; Cash & Ledgers/Accounting (5.A.15/5.A.19) for journals | None | `portfolio.captable.edit`; `portfolio.captable.approve` (CIO/CFO/admin); creator ≠ approver |
| 5.D.7 Governance | Define reserved matters and thresholds per company; receive consent requests; assign investor review/board review; request information; decide (approve/reject with reason); record board calendar entries and observer attendance; track board actions; receive board packs | Private Markets › company › Governance; Research & IC (5.A.6) for investment committee decisions | None | `portfolio.governance.decide` |
| 5.D.8 Signatures | Create signature requests for company signatories (funding documents, resolutions, acknowledgements); configure each company's signing authority; countersign for the fund; remind, void, download certificate | Private Markets › company › Documents/Signatures; Document Vault (5.A.13) | Publish document with `requiresSignature` creates client signature requests (investor scope) | `documents.edit` + `portfolio.signatures.manage` |
| 5.D.9 Capital & Procurement Requests | Review the queue of company requests (SLA 48 h); ask drill-down questions; approve (VC CFO/executive gate), reject or return with reason; manage commitment and tranches (committed amount, approved/disbursed disbursements); convert approved requests to sourcing (RFQ/PO) or release funds; mark fulfilled; review goods-received notes | Private Markets › company › Requests; Operations/Cash for disbursement (5.A.15/5.A.16) | None (Nexus has capital events for investors only) | `portfolio.requests.review`; `portfolio.requests.approve` (executive) |
| 5.D.10 Document Vault | Publish documents to a company (existing publish flow with `companyId`), set access profile and folder, request documents from the company, withdraw/supersede, see company uploads (excluding Company-only) | Document Vault (5.A.13) with a company scope selector; Private Markets › company › Documents | `documents` publish to clients; company library read | `documents.edit`, `documents.view` |
| 5.D.11 Messages | Work an inbox per team queue (Portfolio, Legal & Governance, Value Creation, Fund Finance); reply, assign, resolve, reopen (decision), attach files | Private Markets › company › Messages and a global "Company messages" queue | Raise question, reply, resolve (company questions) | `research.edit` |
| 5.D.12 Team & Access | Invite and disable company users, set initial access profile, view the external-access log, certify access review | Private Markets › company › Team; RBAC & Controls (5.A.26) for policy | Invite company user | `research.edit` (invite), `admin.rbac` (policy) |
| 5.D.13 Settings | Maintain legal name, registration number, tax number, fund relationship; approve company-submitted letterhead/logo changes; set reminder defaults | Private Markets › company › Profile | None (company has no update endpoint) | `research.edit` |

Staff data flows required for each company page to show real data: (1) open period → Reporting/KPI/Forecast; (2) KPI definitions → KPI Centre; (3) term sheet issued + finalised → Term Sheet/Signatures; (4) cap table published → Cap Table; (5) consent decision → Governance; (6) request decision/drill-down → Requests; (7) document published → Vault; (8) staff message → Messages; (9) member invited → Team.

Proposed candidate permission codes (not seeded; Q-04): `portfolio.terms.issue`, `portfolio.captable.edit`, `portfolio.captable.approve`, `portfolio.requests.review`, `portfolio.requests.approve`, `portfolio.governance.decide`, `portfolio.forecast.review`, `portfolio.signatures.manage`, `portfolio.kpi.define`.

Segregation of duties to enforce on the staff side: reporting review (reviewer ≠ submitter, exists), cap-table transaction (creator ≠ approver, to add), term-sheet issue vs fund signature (issuer ≠ signatory, to decide), request approval (requester is the company, approver is staff — fund-side dual control above a threshold, Q-NEW), KPI definition change (maker ≠ checker).
