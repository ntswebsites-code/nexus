##### B. Counterpart facts that constrain the Nexus design

- Capital call to LP visibility: in the reference backend a call is visible to the LP only after "send notices" stamps `noticesSentAt`; in Nexus the equivalent point is `POST /capital-events/:id/publish` (status Due), which has no separation from the maker or checker. FR-LP-110: publishing a call or distribution to LPs requires the approver permission (`cash.approve`) and step-up — AC: a user who is neither approver nor holder of the publish permission receives 403.
- `capital_events` carries one amount per client and event; the LP contract needs one call with many investor allocations (call number, `paid`, `outstanding`, `acknowledged_at`). FR-LP-111: introduce `capital_calls` and `capital_call_allocations` and keep `capital_events` as the investor-facing projection (Q-NEW) — AC: two investors of one call see the same call number and different amounts.
- The Nexus `service_requests.status` vocabulary has four values and any transition is allowed; the LP vocabulary has eight. FR-LP-112: store the LP vocabulary and map the Nexus labels for the legacy investor routes — AC: a staff PATCH to `AWAITING_INVESTOR` shows "Awaiting Investor" in the LP table.
- Documents: Nexus publishes by `clientIds` with maker-checker approval; the LP contract adds category, fund and period. FR-LP-113: add `fund_id`, `category`, `period` to `documents` (or a view) — AC: the LP document list filters by category and fund.
- Q-156: Does LP document publication follow the Nexus approval workflow (draft, in review, approved, published) or publish immediately as in the reference backend? — default: Nexus workflow (`document_approval` policy).
- Q-157: Who owns fund master data, share classes and dealing rules in Nexus (Private Markets page, NAV & Valuation page, or a new Funds page)? — default: a new "Funds" administration page under 5.A.3 with maker-checker, because all LP screens depend on it.

**C.2 Counterparts NOT found in code (gaps the SRD must treat as "staff UI/API to build")**

1. Process a dealing request: no endpoint/service to move `LpDealingRequest.status` (compliance review, awaiting funds, funds received, awaiting NAV, allocate, reject, cancel), nor to apply the outcome to `LpHoldingPosition` (units/NAV/account value) or post a subscription/redemption to the GL or ledger.
2. Maintain `LpHoldingPosition` (units, NAV/unit, account value, as-of, valuation status, share class, account reference) — e.g. NAV import / approval.
3. Maintain `LpDealingRules` (min balance, notice days, suspension, fees, next dealing date, rulesJson frequency/lag) and `LpFundPortalContext` (operating model, currency, share class, valuation status, as-of, public reference).
4. Maintain `LpComplianceStatus` (KYC, accredited, hold) and `LpBankAccount`.
5. Import/approve benchmark series (`LpBenchmarkSeriesPoint`, incl. `approvedBy/approvedAt`).
6. Set valuation status per fund/holding (PROVISIONAL/ESTIMATED/RESTATED) and snapshot override/approval; produce `approvedBy`/`version` metadata (code emits constants).
7. Review LP payment confirmations (`LpCapitalCallPaymentConfirmation`), match them to bank receipts, and trigger `recordAllocationPayment`; no queue, notification or UI hook exists.
8. Cancel/reissue a capital call; DRAFT + maker-checker approval before LP visibility (the design SRD §2.14); there is no `CANCELLED/DRAFT/ISSUED` writer.
9. Attach a notice/statement PDF to an allocation (possible indirectly via `POST /lp-portal/admin/documents` with source refs; capital-call/distribution notice PDFs generated by staff services are emailed, not stored in `LpPortalDocument`).
10. Realtime/notification emission when a call is noticed, a payment recorded, a distribution paid or a dealing request changes status.
11. Performance report job processor (async generation producing a downloadable file) and job failure handling.
12. Audit-event store for LP actions (the design SRD §21).

**C.3 Staff UI a Nexus admin portal would need (derived from the above)**

- Capital calls (per fund): create call (callPercent, due date, transaction date, bank instructions JSON/text), preview allocations (commitment, uncalled-before, call amount), send notices with per-recipient dispatch report, record payments per LP allocation (amount, date, cash GL), view per-allocation payment ledger, view/review LP payment confirmations (file, sha256, claimed amount, notes), cancel/draft/approve workflow (new).
- Distributions (per fund): declare (date, source, gross, carry override, linked dividend/company, notes), preview waterfall (gross, hurdle, carry, net to LPs, per-LP share), send notices, record payouts per LP, per-LP destination bank.
- Dealing desk: queue of subscription/redemption requests with estimate snapshot, compliance gate result, status transitions (compliance review, awaiting funds, funds received, awaiting NAV, allocate/reject/cancel), NAV-per-unit capture and allocation to holdings, dealing-rule editor (min balance, notice, frequency/next dealing date, fees, suspension), compliance status editor (KYC, accredited, hold + reason), LP bank-account directory.
- Valuation/performance: per-fund portal context (operating model, share class, valuation status, as-of), holdings/NAV import, snapshot refresh trigger + status, benchmark series import/approval, performance report run/schedule (existing fund-reporting endpoints) and job monitor.
- Impersonation ("view as LP") with `?clientId=` for support.
